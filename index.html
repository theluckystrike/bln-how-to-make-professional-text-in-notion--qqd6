How to Make Professional Text in Notion with BeLikeNative Keyboard Shortcut

Originally published at belikenative.com/how-to-make-professional-text-in-notion-with-belikenative-keyboard-shortcut

I’ll be honest with you. I used to cringe at my own Notion notes.

They were messy, full of half-baked thoughts, and I’d often write in a way that sounded like I was texting a friend. Which is fine for personal stuff. But when I had to share a project page or a client update, I’d spend way too long cleaning things up. You know the drill. You type fast, hit enter, and then later you’re stuck rewriting sentences.

That’s where the whole idea of professional text comes in. It’s not about sounding like a robot. It’s about being clear, concise, and a little more polished than your first draft. And if you’re using Notion like I do, you probably want to get there without opening a separate app or switching tabs constantly.

Let me show you how I fixed this problem. And spoiler alert, it involves a keyboard shortcut that changed my workflow.

Why Your Notion Notes Look Like Garbage (And Mine Did Too)

Look, we all type like we talk. That’s fine for brainstorming. But when you’re building a knowledge base, a to-do list for your team, or even a personal journal entry you want to look back on, sloppy text feels bad. It’s like wearing pajamas to a meeting. Comfortable, but not ideal.

I’ve seen people write entire project briefs in Notion with no punctuation. Or they use the same word five times in two sentences. It’s not that they’re bad writers. They’re just in a rush. And Notion, for all its strengths, doesn’t have a built-in magic button that rewrites your messy thoughts into something professional.

That’s the gap. You have the content. You just need the polish.

So how do you bridge that gap without spending ten minutes per paragraph? You use a tool that works right inside your browser, right where you’re typing.

The One Shortcut That Changed My Writing

I found this tool called BeLikeNative a few months ago. It’s a Chrome extension that sits quietly in the background until you need it. And the magic happens with a simple keyboard shortcut.

You write your rough draft in Notion. It can be sloppy. It can have typos. It can even be just bullet points. Then you highlight the text, hit the shortcut, and the tool rewrites it into professional, clear language. It keeps your voice, but it fixes the grammar, tightens the sentences, and makes you sound like you actually proofread.

I use it mostly for client-facing Notion pages. But I also use it for my own notes when I want them to be searchable and readable later. It’s like having an editor on call, but without the awkward feedback.

Here’s a real example. I had a note that said: “Need to talk to Sarah about the launch date, also check if the budget is approved, and maybe we should push it back a week.”

Sounds like a normal thought, right? After running it through BeLikeNative, it became: “I need to discuss the launch date with Sarah. I also need to confirm budget approval. We may need to delay the launch by one week.”

Same meaning. Same voice. But now it reads like a professional action item. No extra effort from me.

A Mini Case Study: How I Fixed My Team’s Project Board

I manage a small team of five. We use Notion as our central hub. Every Monday, everyone posts their weekly updates. And every Monday, I’d spend twenty minutes rewriting their updates so they were clear and consistent.

It was exhausting.

So I showed them BeLikeNative for Chrome. I told them to write their updates however they wanted. No pressure. Then, before posting, hit the shortcut. The results surprised me.

Within two weeks, our project board looked completely different. No more run-on sentences. No more confusing bullet points. The updates were crisp and professional. One team member told me it saved them about ten minutes per week. And for me, I stopped being the designated rewriter.

Here’s a quick breakdown of what changed:

1. **Faster communication.** Everyone got to the point faster. No more fluff. 2. **Fewer misunderstandings.** Clear text meant fewer follow-up questions. 3. **Better team morale.** Nobody felt judged for their writing style anymore. 4. **Consistent voice.** The project board looked like it was written by one person, not five.

That last point matters more than you think. When your Notion pages look consistent, they feel more trustworthy. Clients notice. Stakeholders notice. And honestly, it just feels good.

Does This Actually Make You a Better Writer?

Here’s my personal opinion. No tool can replace real writing skill. But tools like this can make you look more competent than you feel in the moment. And that’s valuable.

I’ve been writing for a long time. I still make mistakes. I still write sloppy first drafts. The difference is now I don’t have to stare at a paragraph for five minutes trying to figure out why it sounds weird. I just highlight and press the shortcut. It’s like having a safety net.

Some people worry that using AI writing tools makes you lazy. I disagree. It makes you efficient. You still have to know what you want to say. The tool just helps you say it better.

One stat that stuck with me: approximately 70% of professionals say they spend extra time rewriting emails or notes to sound more polished. That’s time you could spend on actual work. Or on your lunch break. Your choice.

How to Set It Up So It Feels Natural

You don’t need a technical degree for this. Here’s the simple process I followed.

First, install the extension from the Chrome Web Store. It takes about ten seconds. Then, open any Notion page. Type something, anything. Even a grocery list. Highlight a sentence or two. Press the shortcut. Mine is set to Alt+Shift+P, but you can change it in the settings.

The first time you use it, you’ll probably laugh. It’s that fast. And the rewrite feels like you, just smarter. I recommend trying it on a few different types of text. A long paragraph. A short email draft. A to-do list. See how it handles each one.

I personally use it most for meeting notes. I jot down quick thoughts during a call, then after the call, I run them through the shortcut. Suddenly my meeting notes are clean, organized, and ready to share. No extra work.

What About the Fear of Losing Your Voice?

This is a real concern. I get it. You don’t want your Notion pages to sound like a corporate brochure. You want them to sound like you.

The good news is that BeLikeNative doesn’t strip away your personality. It just removes the noise. Your opinions, your humor, your specific phrasing, those stay. What disappears is the awkward grammar, the repetitive words, and the sentences that trail off into nowhere.

I tested this with a friend who writes very casually. She used the shortcut on a personal journal entry. The result was cleaner, but it still sounded like her. She actually said, “Oh, so that’s what I was trying to say.” That’s the sweet spot.

One More Thing About Notion Workflows

Notion is great for collecting ideas. But it’s not great at editing them on the fly. That’s why having a keyboard shortcut feels like a superpower. You don’t leave the page. You don’t copy and paste into another tool. You just fix it where you are.

I’ve started using this for everything. Client proposals. Team updates. Even my personal reading notes. It’s made me more confident about sharing my Notion pages with others. And honestly, it’s made me enjoy writing more. Because now I don’t dread the editing part.

If you’ve been avoiding Notion because your writing feels messy, or if you’re tired of rewriting the same things over and over, give this shortcut a try. It’s free to start. And it might just change the way you think about professional writing.

You don’t have to be a natural writer. You just have to know how to use the right tools.
